Relationship-Aware Domain Randomization

An exploratory direction asking how physical relationships among robot parameters could inform domain randomization.

Motivation & research questions

Independent parameter randomization may create combinations of dynamics that are physically unrealistic or unusually difficult. I’m interested in whether respecting relationships among physical parameters can support robust behavior while preserving nominal-task performance.

  • How do correlated physical parameters affect policy robustness?
  • When does independent randomization produce unrealistic or excessively challenging dynamics?
  • Could structured randomization reduce overly conservative control behavior?
  • How should robustness and nominal-task performance be assessed together?

Proposed investigation

  • Examine physical relationships among parameters used in randomized simulation.
  • Investigate structured sampling that preserves relevant parameter correlations.
  • Consider comparisons with independent parameter randomization.
  • Study the relationship between robustness, control conservatism, and nominal performance.

Methods & tools

The direction concerns reinforcement learning, robot dynamics, simulation, and domain randomization. A specific simulator, robot, implementation, and experimental protocol have not been established here.

Status & limitations

This is an exploratory research direction, not a completed method or an established published contribution.

The possible benefits of preserving physical relationships are research questions. No improvement in robustness, transfer, or nominal performance is claimed.
