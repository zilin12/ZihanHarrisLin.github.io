Publications

A Stereo Vision-Based Datum Detection Method for Mobile Machining Robots

Z. Lin, Z. Xie, S. Shentu, F. Xie, and X.-J. Liu

IEEE 16th Int. Conf. on CYBER Technology in Automation, Control, and Intelligent Systems (CYBER), Florence, Italy, 2026, pp. 363–368CYBER 2026

A training-free stereo-vision pipeline that detects datum features on reflective aluminum parts and builds part coordinate systems for a 5-DOF mobile parallel machining robot. It detected every datum vertex in all 12 test images of 6 parts with >95% IoU in under 0.35 s per part, and the robot completed a full machining cycle from the detected frame.

Learning-Based Manipulation on ALOHA 2

Senior capstone · Team lead · Industry sponsor: Factory Intelligence · Aug 2026 – Present

  • Lead an 8-person team comparing learning approaches for manipulation; built the main pipeline on SkipVLA (CoMMA Lab), pairing a classical planner for free-space motion with a VLA policy for contact-rich steps.
  • Advise imitation-learning and LLM-agent subteams; preliminary three-block stacking in simulation, adding domain randomization ahead of hardware deployment; final milestone is shelf placement.
  • Profiled training on a single RTX 5080: domain randomization doubled demonstration-recording and imitation-fit time, and over 80% of each RL iteration went to CPU episode collection rather than the ~2 s GPU update.

MDP-Mahjong: Open-Source Robot Mahjong System

Personal project

  • Built an end-to-end system in which a robot arm plays one seat of simplified Chinese Mahjong, from discard selection to physical tile placement in MuJoCo.
  • Trained a recurrent PPO agent with curriculum and league play: 29.8% win rate against a held-out heuristic opponent (random play: 0.07%), with zero illegal actions.
  • Ablated a learned wrist-camera contact correction against open-loop classical contact (71 vs. 84 of 100 hands) and deselected it after out-of-distribution failures; the final system completes 97 of 100 hands in simulation.
